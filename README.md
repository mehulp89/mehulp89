## Hello, I'm Mehul 👋

I'm a **Mobile Architect and Android Engineering Manager** with **14+ years of experience** turning complex product requirements into scalable mobile platforms.

My work sits at the intersection of **Android, IoT, cloud-connected devices, and real-time video**. I enjoy solving the hard problems behind the interface: architecture that can evolve, SDKs that other teams love using, reliable device communication, smooth streaming, measurable performance, and engineering practices that help people ship with confidence.

Today, I focus equally on **systems and people**—setting technical direction, simplifying complexity, mentoring engineers, and building teams that can own ambitious products end to end.

> Great architecture is not about adding more layers. It is about making the right changes easier, safer, and faster.

## What I do best

- **Mobile architecture** — designing modular Android systems with clear boundaries, predictable data flow, and long-term maintainability.
- **Engineering leadership** — aligning teams around technical strategy, quality standards, ownership, and pragmatic delivery.
- **IoT platforms** — connecting apps with cameras, smart devices, cloud services, and device-control workflows.
- **Real-time media** — building responsive video experiences with RTSP, WebRTC, streaming, playback, and network-aware behavior.
- **SDK & platform engineering** — creating stable abstractions and reusable capabilities that accelerate multiple products and teams.
- **Performance & reliability** — improving startup, responsiveness, memory use, observability, resilience, and release confidence.

### How I approach engineering

```text
Understand the product → Model the system → Make trade-offs explicit
        → Build for change → Measure in production → Improve continuously
```

I value **clarity over cleverness**, **evidence over assumptions**, and **sustainable engineering over short-lived velocity**.
